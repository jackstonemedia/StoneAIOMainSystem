## What changed (M7 new work)

- **New service `api/services/email-marketing/cold-outreach.service.ts`**:
  - `coldDailyCap`: 20/day days 1–3, 40/day days 4–7, 75/day week 2, 100/day
    after (spec §13, "defaults (assumptions to tune)").
  - `coldDayNumber` (day 1 = first-send day), `coldRoomToday` (cap minus
    SENT + claimed rows stamped today, UTC day), `coldSentToday`,
    `coldAnchor` (campaign sentAt, else earliest SENT, else now).
  - `validateColdFormat`: images (`<img`) → blocking; bodyText word count
    outside 50–100 → blocking (count includes the whole body text).
  - `withinColdWindow`: weekdays 8:00–17:00 in the business timezone
    (Intl-based; unknown timezone never blocks on a formatting guess).
  - `coldShouldSkip`: suppression entry, unsubscribed/bounced/complained
    membership, exited RecipientState, or a reply signal (via
    `detectExitSignals`, which excludes auto-replies via AUTO_REPLY_HINTS —
    so auto-replies do NOT stop a cold campaign, per spec).
  - `processColdDrip` (30s tick): for SENT cold campaigns with PENDING
    recipients, inside the send window: reclaims stale claims (>2h, e.g.
    after a restart) to PENDING, claims up to today's room atomically
    (PENDING → SENDING with sentAt set at claim time, so a claimed row
    counts toward the cap before its send lands), skips exited/suppressed
    contacts (SKIPPED:<reason>), sends the rest with a short random delay
    between sends, and enrolls truly-SENT recipients into multi-email
    sequences (per-person timing from their own first send — the
    SENT-transition enrollment only covers recipients who were already SENT,
    so dripped recipients would otherwise never get follow-ups).
  - `coldSendContext`: one gate per send invocation (detect once, re-check
    room before every send, human jitter between cold sends).
- **Send path (`campaigns.service.ts`)**: `sendCampaign` (all three loops:
  variant A, variant B, standard broadcast) and `resumeAndCompleteSending`
  check the cold gate before every send; at-cap recipients stay PENDING
  (never failed) for the drip tick. Cold broadcasts send in shuffled order.
  Cold sends wait the existing human jitter (30–90s) between sends.
- **Worker (`email-send-worker.service.ts`)**: cold campaigns send plain
  text only — no HTML part. `sendGmailMessage` gained a `textOnly` flag
  backed by a pure, unit-tested `buildGmailMime` helper (default shape
  unchanged: multipart/alternative).
- **Phase 8 checks (`send-orchestration.service.ts`)**: cold-format blocking
  items (images, word count) alongside the existing ack blocking item.
- **Tick (`email-marketing.routes.ts`)**: `processColdDrip` on the 30s
  interval with its own try/catch (`[ColdDrip]`).
- **UI**: cold ramp note in the ReviewSend checks section when the draft is
  cold (ramp schedule + US-only + separate-domain recommendation + stop on
  reply). The acknowledgment checkbox itself was already there.

## Cold + A/B interaction (handled, not ignored)

but before the drip sends it, the drip flips that recipient to the winner
variant before sending (split mode sends both variants fully, so no flip
there). Code-reviewed; not live-verified (needs Gmail + a multi-day run).

## Bug found and fixed during live verification

The drip counted a send as "dripped" from `processEmailSendJobDirectly`'s
`ok` flag — but that flag resolves true even when the worker marked the
recipient FAILED (its last-attempt path resolves after marking; pre-existing
semantics shared with sendCampaign's queued count). A FAILED drip send
would have wrongly enrolled the recipient into a sequence. Fixed: the drip
re-reads the recipient row and enrolls only when status is actually SENT
(and uses that row's sentAt for per-person timing). Re-verified by
typecheck + full suite; the drip's cap/skip behavior was verified live
before the fix and is unaffected by it (claims, room, and skip checks are
independent of the enrollment guard).

## Deviations / known limitations

- US-only is enforced by attestation, not by a code-level country filter:
  Contacts and CSV rows carry no reliable country field, so there is nothing
  to filter on. The acknowledgment checkbox (spec copy, blocking) is the
  attestation, and the ramp note states US-only. A future enhancement could
  add a country column to contacts/CSV and filter on it.
- Randomized spacing is: shuffled recipient order + 30–90s human jitter
  between initial sends + short random delays between drip sends + the
  daily caps spreading sends across days. Sends are NOT spread across the
  hours of the send window (a future enhancement could schedule each drip
  send at a random time inside the window).
- Real delivery UNVERIFIED (no Gmail in dev): drip sends fail honestly
  without Gmail; the cap/claim/skip/enrollment logic is verified to the
  send-attempt point. Text-only MIME is unit-verified (no HTML part, no
  boundary, no images); the actual Gmail transmission of a text-only cold
  email awaits a connected Gmail account.
- A campaign paused mid-drip leaves claimed (SENDING) rows until they go
  stale (>2h) and are reclaimed on the next drip run after resume.
- The drip tick only runs inside the send window; PENDING recipients wait
  for the next window day if the cap or window blocks them.
- Cold + A/B winner flip in the drip is code-reviewed, not live-verified.
