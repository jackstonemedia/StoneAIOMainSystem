  (`'gemini-2.5-flash'` → `DEFAULT_MODEL`). Everything else that used
  `DEFAULT_MODEL` (ai-copilot, ai routes, workflow-ai, SDR) picks the new

## Bugs found by live verification and fixed in M2

1. **Forward reopen stranded a draft** in a phase with no checklist (reopen to
   an unvisited phase). Fixed: `reopenPhase` now rejects anything but a
   strictly backward move (`canReopen`, unit-tested); the UI only ever offers
   finished phases for Back.
2. **A locked Brief could be re-locked** (confirm-brief checked phase + brief
   but not status, so a second "looks good" bumped the version). Fixed:
   confirm-brief now requires status `interviewing` → 400 "This Brief is
   already confirmed."
3. **StrictMode double-effect created duplicate blank drafts** in dev (two
   concurrent loads both saw an empty list). Fixed: `autoCreateRef` guard in
   `InterviewFlow` (resets on create failure).
4. **Profile-change banner was suppressed on brief-confirmed drafts** even
   though the regenerate/keep choice only swaps the snapshot and never touches
   the locked Brief. Fixed: the banner now shows regardless of confirmation
   status (unit-tested).

## Test script for Jack

1. Open Email Marketing → Create with AI → "Looks right, continue".
2. Type an answer to the Goal question and Send. (With a real key in play,
   the AI asks exactly one follow-up in plain words; with the dev stub key you
   see the honest "AI is not configured" error and your answer stays saved —
   click Retry once AI is configured.)
3. Click "Write it with what I have" to skip a phase — missing details become
   placeholders and the progress bar advances.
4. Reload the page: the resume list shows your draft — Resume it, or start
   a new campaign (every campaign starts blank).
5. Hatch through Call to Action to the Campaign Brief, type "looks good",
   Send — the Brief locks. Type anything else instead and the interview
   re-opens Phase 3 with your correction saved.
6. Change the Business Profile, reopen the draft: a banner offers
   "Regenerate draft" or "Keep as is".
7. Confirm there are no buttons, chips, dropdowns, or suggested answers to tap
   anywhere in the interview — only typing (or Dictate, where supported).
