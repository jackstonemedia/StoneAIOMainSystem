The product tour is gone entirely. No auto-launch, no TourLauncher/TourProvider/overlay/tooltip, no tour registry or definitions, no Command Palette tour entries, no Getting Started hub, no onboarding-progress API, no `UserOnboardingProgress` model, and no `data-tour` anchors left in any page.
